# One-Line Roast candidates — "One-Line Roast: The Gadget You Use Every Day"

Bounty: u-daring-gecko-25-one-line-roast-the-gadget-you-use-3d6aa610
($5 pot, top 3 paid $2/$2/$1; closes 26 Nov 2026 15:00 UTC / 22:00 WIB)

Judge brief: funniest, freshest, neatest single line (≤200 chars) roasting a
gadget/app used every day. Avoid the sponsor's own examples (phone dying at
4pm, app demanding a rating, smart kettle firmware update) — those will be
the most-copied angles in the field.

## Candidates (all drafted fresh in-session, 24 Nov 2026)

1. My smartwatch congratulates me for standing up, like I just climbed Everest instead of getting off the couch to get crisps.
2. My earbuds die mid-sentence on every important call, then reconnect the second the other person hangs up.
3. My printer only works when I don't need it, and the one time I do, it develops a sudden allergy to paper.
4. My phone's face unlock doesn't recognise me before coffee, which is fair — neither do I.
5. My Wi-Fi router needs restarting every Tuesday, like it has a standing appointment to ruin my video call.
6. My microwave beeps five times when it's done, as if the first four were just a warm-up for my impatience.
7. My robot vacuum eats phone chargers for breakfast, then gets stuck under the couch and begs for rescue.
8. My laptop fan sounds like a jet taking off every time I open a spreadsheet, as if the numbers were heavy.
9. My smart speaker answers questions nobody asked, then goes deaf the moment I actually say its name.
10. My car beeps angrily about the seatbelt on an empty passenger seat, like my grocery bag is in mortal danger.
11. My alarm lets me snooze nine times, then acts betrayed when I'm late, as if it didn't help me do it.
12. My fitness tracker counted 10,000 steps while I was driving, so apparently my car is the athlete now.
13. My smart fridge beeps if the door is open for three seconds, like it's guarding state secrets and not leftover rice.
14. My password manager forgot my master password, which is like a locksmith locking himself out of his own shop.
15. My phone says "storage full" after I delete everything, as if the photos I deleted are still squatting in there.
16. My GPS reroutes me straight into the traffic jam it was supposed to avoid, then says "you have arrived" like it helped.
17. My washing machine plays a three-minute song when it's done, a concert I never asked for at 11pm.
18. My toothbrush app grades my brushing and gave me a C, so now my teeth have homework.
19. My TV remote has 47 buttons and not one of them just turns the thing on.

## Pick

**#1 (smartwatch)** — strongest: absurd contrast (Everest vs. crisps), a
specific behaviour everyone with a watch recognises, tight wording, and an
angle the sponsor's examples don't touch. Runners-up: #12 (fitness tracker
driving), #7 (robot vacuum), #8 (laptop fan as jet).

## Revision note

First draft (#1 as written above) was good but "congratulates me for
standing up" is a little flat. Revised to "throws a party every time I stand
up" — bigger, funnier verb, and "fetching crisps from the kitchen were a
summit of Everest" tightens the punchline. Final line in main.py.
